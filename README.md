# Video X-Ray

**Paste a viral video. Get it back as a recipe. Then get your own version.**

Shot by shot: how each shot was made, the hook, the beat map, the rhythm in
numbers, the sound, why it works. Then the same structure remade with your
face, your topic or your product, cut on the original's measured rhythm,
finished in your gallery.

## What it does

| | |
|---|---|
| **Every cut, measured** | Each shot's start, end and length, how much moves inside it, the pace, whether the cutting speeds up, and how many cuts land on the beat. |
| **One still from inside every shot** | Labelled with its shot number and times, plus the opening frame, plus extra stills inside long shots where the picture changes without a cut. |
| **How each shot was made** | Filmed on a phone, AI video, talking avatar, screen recording, motion graphic, a still with a zoom, or footage from a film, with a confidence and the clue that decided it. |
| **What the file itself says** | A generator that signed its output, an AI label, a content credential, the app or phone that saved it last. |
| **The Viral DNA card** | The idea in one line, the hook second by second, the beat map, what keeps people watching, the sound, the ending, why it works and what is weak. |
| **The remake** | The same beats and rhythm with your content, each shot routed to the right engine, priced before anything is spent, the hook shot made and checked first, then cut on the original's timeline. |
| **Any language** | Reports, on-screen text and voice in your language. Hebrew right to left included. |

## Install

Video X-Ray is a skill. The X-ray itself is free; making the new video uses
Cadabra AI's engines, so you need a Cadabra AI package:
https://www.livinginmovie.com/pricing

**Claude Code**

```bash
claude plugin marketplace add MeitalSela/video-xray
claude plugin install video-xray@video-xray
```

The plugin connects the Cadabra AI connector for you. Sign in when Claude
asks.

**Claude (web or desktop) and ChatGPT**

1. Add the Cadabra AI connector: `https://mcp.livinginmovie.com/mcp`.
   Step by step: https://www.livinginmovie.com/en/guides/cadabra-mcp
2. Add the skill: download [`skills/video-xray/SKILL.md`](skills/video-xray/SKILL.md)
   and upload it in your skills settings, or paste it into a project's
   instructions.

Then say:

```text
X-ray this video and make me one like it: <link to the video file>
```

## How to give it a video

The connector needs a direct link to the video file. Save the TikTok, Reel
or Short from the app, then upload it at https://livinginmovie.com/mcp/upload
(or attach it in ChatGPT) and paste the link back into the chat.

## Use it fairly

Study a video's structure, pacing and techniques. Do not re-post other
people's footage, music, characters, brands or faces. The remake is made
from material you own or generate. Label AI content where the platform asks
you to.
